Execution Story 02

Demand Spike Managed

A sudden demand signal was converted into an executable capacity shift without waiting for a full planning cycle.

14 min

Operational response time

48 hr

Planning cycle avoided

Multi-node

Signals evaluated together

DeciOps combined social, logistics, demand, and fulfillment signals to identify the spike early and evaluate options for supply allocation and capacity balancing.

The recommended action was executed through governed automation, with the decision log preserved for audit and learning.
