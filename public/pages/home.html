Supply Chains Have Become Adaptive

In an era of global volatility and instant customer expectations, the static nature of enterprise systems has become a bottleneck. Traditional architectures were designed for stable transactions and batch planning.

Today, operational teams are overwhelmed by factors they can't control: cascading backorders, inventory conflicts, fulfillment delays, and fragmented vendor responses. Visibility isn't enough; continuous decision execution is the new requirement.
