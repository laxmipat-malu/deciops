1. Overview

DeciOps™ (DeciOps.ai), operated by Inferonix Systems Pvt Ltd, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our platform, products, and enterprise solutions.

Our technology is designed for the Sovereign Engineer—providing advanced AI orchestration without compromising the integrity of your proprietary architectural frameworks or data streams.

2. Information We Collect

Direct Input

We collect information you provide directly, including name, enterprise email, billing credentials, and any operational metadata uploaded to the platform.

System Telemetry

This includes technical data such as IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and system-level performance logs generated during platform usage.

Log_Stream
