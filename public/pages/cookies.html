Overview

Inferonix Systems Pvt Ltd utilizes sophisticated data structures to orchestrate your enterprise experience. We view cookies not as mere tracking tools, but as essential navigational anchors that synchronize your decision-making workflows across the deciops.ai domain and the DeciOps™ platform.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files deployed to facilitate recognition, remember your preferences, and track performance. These data packets reside on your hardware, serving as a persistent handshake between your terminal and our core architecture.

We utilize cookies for core functions, understanding platform usage, performance improvement, maintaining secure sessions, and ensuring content relevance. By analyzing these interaction points, we refine the predictive accuracy of our intelligence engine.

Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary

Mandatory for the integrity of the environment. This includes secure login state, platform navigation, and core features that cannot be bypassed without operational failure.

Performance

Anonymous tracking of visits and interaction patterns to identify performance issues and optimize system throughput.

Functionality

Remembering your preferences, customization settings, and regional offsets for a tailored interface.

Targeting & Analytics

Monitoring content effectiveness and user behavior to ensure communication relevance and recommended intelligence modules.

05 Third-Party

We collaborate with elite analytics and integration toolchains. These third-party entities may deploy their own identifiers to track performance metrics, governed by their respective privacy policies.

06 Disabled States

Should you choose to restrict cookie deployment, please be advised of potential loss of functionality, limited navigation, and loss of personalization features across the DeciOps™ experience.

07 Managing Cookies

Management is maintained via your local browser settings. You can block or delete cookies, or set alerts for when cookies are being sent, retaining sovereignty over your data lifecycle.

08 Existing Cookies

Existing cookies may remain in storage post-denial, but no new collection parameters will be initiated. Manual purging via browser settings is required to clear historical data remnants.
